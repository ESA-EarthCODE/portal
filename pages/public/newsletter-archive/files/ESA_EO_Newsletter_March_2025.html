Highlights

EarthCODE: NEW CALL OPEN

EarthCODE is a new ESA EO initiative to ensure long-term access and reuse of  Earth System Science data and methods (code). Integrating EO platform services based on various technologies such as pangeo stack and openEO, it empowers scientists to adopt FAIR Open Science and publish reproducible research. A call for new subcontractors is open until 7 April 2025. 

HOW TO APPLY

ESA APEx: SERVICES TO STREAMLINE EO INNOVATION TO OPERATIONS

The ESA Application Propagation Environments (APEx) initiative transforms Earth observation outcomes into scalable, reusable on-demand services. With FAIR principles, it offers tailored support ensuring seamless integration into cloud platforms, APEx guarantees long-term accessibility, usability and continued impact through the ESA Network of Resources

NEW OPEN DATA ON FIRE EMISSIONS

A new open access dataset from SENSE4FIRE study funded by ESA EO is available at TUD’s OPARA data repository. The dataset was derived and analysed in the Nature Geoscience paper by Forkel et al. (2024) which showed that smouldering woody debris in the Amazon and Cerrado biomes contribute up to 75% of burned biomass, releasing 40 million tonnes of CO2 during the 2020 wildfires.

ESA, NASA and JAXA satellites observing the record breaking El Nino 2023-2024

Open data from ESA, NASA and JAXA EO missions help understand and characterise El Niño and its global consequences. Learn about the fifth most powerful El Niño  in recorded history from two new stories (Part 1 and Part 2) recently published on the tri-agency EO Dashboard - a great resource for education in EO science.

JOIN THE EARTH SYSTEM SCIENCE HUB

The Earth System Science Hub is a new ESA facility for advanced Earth system and climate science studies. It is a centre of excellence for science collaboration, networking, and exchange of ideas, promoting a community response to major science challenges of this decades. Discover the diverse opportunities to get involved!

A NEW MOOC ON EO OPEN SCIENCE

ESA has developed several Massive Open Online Courses (MOOCs), with more to come in the future. The Massive Open Online Course 'Cubes & Clouds - Cloud Native Open Data Sciences for Earth Observation' teaches the concepts of data cubes, cloud platforms and open science in the context of Earth observation.
