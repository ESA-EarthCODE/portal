DISCOVER THE NEW RACE HOMEPAGE

RACE (Rapid Action for Citizens with Earth Observation) is a collaborative Earth Observation innovation initiative by ESA and the European Commission - DG DEFIS, bringing together environmental, economic, and societal indicators developed by European industry, research institutes, and academia, based on ESA and Copernicus Earth Observation data. Explore these indicators and success stories through the RACE's brand new and user-friendly homepage and its interactive dashboard.

HORIZON EUROPE 2026: EU-ESA SCIENCE CALLS OPEN

New upcoming opportunities in 2026 and 2027 for collaborative science under the Earth System Science initiative (ESSI) between ESA and EC- DG-RTD. Horizon Europe Proposals using satellite remote sensing data and derived products are encouraged to collaborate with projects selected under ESA’s FutureEO programme and the EC-ESA ESSI to facilitate access to and uptake of satellite data products and related tools or methodologies.

Explore the newly released cloud-optimized WAPOSAL dataset for Wave Energy research

The WAPOSAL (Wave Power & Satellite Altimetry) project released a new cloud-optimized dataset for coastal wave energy assessment, openly accessible via the ESA Open Science Catalogue. Derived from Sentinel-3A/B and CryoSat-2 altimetry (2011–2022), it provides wave power density, zero-crossing wave period, and significant wave height at 300 m resolution. Coverage spans European Atlantic coasts, the Mediterranean and Baltic Seas, and island regions including Madeira, the Azores, the Canary Islands, and French Polynesia. Supported by the EarthCODE data engineering team, WAPOSAL is distributed in a cloud-ready format, enabling easy access for the coastal altimetry community. This resource supports renewable energy research and Europe’s 2050 climate-neutral goals by leveraging satellite altimetry for high-resolution wave energy assessments.

New ESA-NASA-JAXA EO Dashboard story explores Urban Health with interactive indicators and notebook


A new urban health story featured on the ESA-NASA-JAXA EO Dashboard shows how satellite data can be used to monitor urban-related indicators in New Delhi, one of the world’s most densely populated megacities. The story combines Earth Observation data and socio-economic indicators to track extreme heat, air pollution, human activity, and multidimensional deprivation. Users can explore these insights interactively through the EO Dashboard and reproduce analyses via the accompanying Jupyter Notebook, demonstrating how open satellite data can support urban resilience and public health planning.
