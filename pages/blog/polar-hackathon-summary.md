# Polar Hackathon Success! <!--{ as="img" mode="hero" src="https://earthcode.esa.int/img/Antarctica_In_Sync_visualisation.jpeg?raw=true" }-->
## Polar Hackathon Success!
*Published: 29.07.2026*

The **EarthCODE Polar Hackathon**, held online in the last week of June 2026, brought together 15 participants from the **ESA Polar Science Cluster** community. Led by the ESA Polar Cluster and supported by EarthCODE, the event showcased:
- ESA Antarctic datasets,
- Polar Cluster data cubes,
- Workflows for converting datasets into cloud-native formats,
- Combining datasets into analysis-ready collections, and
- Integrating these collections into scientific workflows.

All the polar datasets are open and accessible via the EarthCODE [Open Science Catalog](https://opensciencedata.esa.int/), with each dataset paired with a guidance notebook to support analysis.

## Hackathon Outcomes
Key highlights from the event include:
- Discussions on the advantages and drawbacks of cloud-optimised datasets and workflows for research purposes.
- Confirmation of strong interest in building a community around ESA data cubes and related datasets, particularly within the Polar Science Cluster.
- Participants actively used the [EarthCODE Discourse Forum](https://earthcode.esa.int/community/) to ask questions, suggest improvements, and troubleshoot technical issues, allowing the EarthCODE team to provide timely support and foster collaboration.
- The Polar Science Cluster are interested in exploring an Arctic data cube as a next step.

## Positive Feedback
Attendees highly valued:
- The Antarctic data cube,
- Example notebooks,
- The opportunity to work directly with ESA EarthCODE datasets.

The Polar Cluster will also use the data cube and notebooks produced during the hackathon at the upcoming Polar Cluster co-location meeting and the Scientific Committee on Antarctic Research (SCAR) Open Science Conference in August.

## Get Involved!
Interested in polar science? The Polar Hackathon materials, including open datasets, tutorials, and notebooks, are freely available to everyone. These resources offer a unique opportunity to:
- Explore cutting-edge polar science data,
- Work with reproducible tools and workflows,
- Engage with a community-driven approach.

Whether you’re a researcher, student, or data enthusiast, these materials are your gateway to discovering Antarctica’s ice shelves, grounding lines, and more. For more information, check out our [blog on the Polar Hackathon](https://earthcode.esa.int/blog/polar-hackathon-polar-data).

EarthCODE will also be organising similar online hackathons for the ESA Ocean and Carbon Science Clusters, to take place in September/October 2026.

All three online hackathons pave the way for the main event: the EarthCODE Hackathon 2026. This hackathon will be an in-person event in ESA ESRIN running from 30 November to 4 December, bringing together researchers, data scientists, and open-data enthusiasts.
